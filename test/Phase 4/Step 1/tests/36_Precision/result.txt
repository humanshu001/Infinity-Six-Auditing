==========================================================================
36_Precision  --  AUDIT.md finding M-7
==========================================================================

What this suite tests
---------------------
`_rpow` (the compounding helper) rounds DOWN on every multiplication.
Over many compounding days the user receives slightly less than the
mathematical expectation. Similarly `_realizeSalary` divides
`rankIncome / 30 days` before multiplying back by elapsed seconds --
a small per-second precision loss biased AGAINST the user.

How it tests it
---------------
Matures a 1,000 USDT package for 365 days and reads
`getTotalLifetimeRWP`. Confirms the result is bounded by the 2.5x
package cap. Also computes the salary-per-second figure for Rank 1
(50 USDT / 30 days) and shows the cumulative 30-day shortfall.

Results
-------
[PASS] test_M7_compound_truncation_after_1_year
  - Principal:                                  1,000 USDT
  - Lifetime RWP at 365 days (capped at 2.5x):  2,500 USDT (= cap)

[PASS] test_M7_salary_per_second_truncation
  - salaryPerSec for Rank 1:                    19,290,123,456,790 wei
  - Sum over 30 days:                           49.99999999999968 USDT
  - Expected (theoretical):                     50.0 USDT
  - Shortfall:                                  320,000 wei (~0.00032 cents)

Plain-English summary
---------------------
These are not exploitable losses, they are dust-level rounding biases
in favor of the protocol. Every cycle, every user, the protocol keeps
a few hundred thousand wei. At scale this adds up to "the house edge"
inherent to integer fixed-point math.

Recommended fix
---------------
Acceptable as-is; document the truncation direction. If user-facing
fairness matters, switch to PRBMath or another higher-precision fixed-
point library and round HALF_UP instead of DOWN.
