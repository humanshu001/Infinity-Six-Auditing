# Reentrancy Attack Simulation Results

The system contract was analyzed for reentrancy vulnerabilities. Two scenarios were evaluated:
1. reentrant investment block: a custom contract attempted to call invest() dynamically using a transfer callback mechanism. the call is rejected immediately due to the protocol's check requiring tx.origin == msg.sender.
2. reentrant withdrawal block: a custom contract attempted to trigger withdraw() dynamically. the call is rejected immediately by the tx.origin check.

All 2 test cases passed successfully. The tx.origin restriction acts as an absolute boundary that blocks smart contract callers from executing reentrancy, and the system contract's nonreentrant modifiers provide secondary security.
