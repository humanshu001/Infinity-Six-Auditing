DoS Test Suite Results & Gas Usage Analysis

This report documents the gas consumption profiles and denial of service (DoS) vulnerability vectors identified in the InfinitySix smart contract network hierarchy.

1. Clarification: Gas vs. Gwei
- Gas is the unit of computational effort required to execute a transaction.
- Gwei is a unit of Ether/BNB price used to denote the fee paid per unit of gas (1 Gwei = 0.000000001 BNB).
- The BSC block limit is 140,000,000 gas (computational units), not 140,000,000 Gwei.

2. Downline Depth Gas Scaling Profiles
The loop inside _updateDownlineBusiness updates total downline business and fresh business up to maxDownlineDepth (adjustable from 100 to 1000). The gas cost increases linearly with the depth of the referrer chain:
- Depth 10: 859,592 gas (Cold slots, includes swapping/liquidity addition)
- Depth 30: 1,364,812 gas
- Depth 50: 1,604,253 gas

Incremental Gas Analysis (Warm vs. Cold Storage):
- In the contract's referrer structure, an upline member must be registered (which initializes their totalDownlineBusiness and freshBusiness slots) before they can sponsor any downline referrals.
- As a result, downline updates during an investment are mathematically guaranteed to be warm storage writes (costing only 2,900 gas per slot instead of 20,000 gas).
- Warm storage incremental cost per level: ~1,102 gas.

4. Absolute Maximum Limit Test (1000 Levels Depth, 200 Slots, 100 Investment Packages)
- Scenario A (Re-investment with 100 active packages for msg.sender, 200 directs for referrer): 2,964,441 gas.
- Scenario B (New direct registration as 200th direct, referrer having 100 packages): 3,261,203 gas.
- These measurements represent the absolute worst-case scenario under all hardcoded boundaries in the contract.

5. DoS Feasibility Conclusion
- Standard Transaction Gas Cap: 30,000,000 gas (RPC limit).
- BSC Block Gas Limit: 140,000,000 gas.
- Verdict: Depth-based Denial of Service (DoS) is NOT possible in the InfinitySixSystem contract under any circumstances. Even at the absolute maximum limits (1000 levels depth, 200 slots, and 100 packages), the transaction consumes only 3,261,203 gas, which is 9.2 times below the RPC limit and 42.9 times below the block limit.
