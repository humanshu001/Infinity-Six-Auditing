==========================================================================
30_GhostVolume  --  AUDIT.md finding H-3
==========================================================================

What this suite tests
---------------------
`_updateDownlineBusiness` increments `totalDownlineBusiness` and
`freshBusiness` on every invest. NEITHER field is ever decremented
when a downline caps, when their package reaches 2.5x, or when they
withdraw. Rank qualifications and rank maintenance metrics therefore
keep climbing from "ghost volume" that is no longer producing income.

How it tests it
---------------
Builds a 6-long downline chain under a sponsor (sponsor -> direct ->
5 sub-chain accounts, each depositing 1,000 USDT). Reads sponsor's
`totalDownlineBusiness` (expect 6 x 1,000 = 6,000 USDT). Matures the
chain a full year and forces each chain member to attempt withdraw to
trigger possible caps. Re-reads the sponsor's metric -- if any
decrement existed it would now be lower.

Results
-------
[PASS] test_H3_totalDownlineBusiness_only_grows
  - totalDownlineBusiness after build:           6,000 USDT
  - freshBusiness after build:                   6,000 USDT
  - After 1 year + every chain member withdraws: 6,000 USDT (UNCHANGED)
  - Ghost volume confirmed.

[PASS] test_H3_no_decrement_path_exists_in_abi
  - No `clearDownlineBusiness()` selector exists.
  - No `recomputeDownlineBusiness()` selector exists.

Plain-English summary
---------------------
Rank qualification is measured against `directDB.totalDeposits +
directDB.totalDownlineBusiness`. Once added, the volume stays counted
forever even if the underlying account hits its 6x cap or simply stops
generating. A user with 200 directs who each cycled through and capped
can still rank up and collect rank salary using purely ghost volume.

Recommended fix
---------------
Symmetric decrement: when a user's `isCapped` flips to true (and on
each package deactivation), walk up `maxDownlineDepth` uplines and
SUBTRACT the contribution from `totalDownlineBusiness` and
`freshBusiness`. The existing `_updateUplineStream(false, ...)` cap-
drop loop is the right shape for this; extend it to also touch the
business counters.

Run command
-----------
forge test --match-path 'test/Phase 4/Step 1/tests/30_GhostVolume/*.sol' -vv
